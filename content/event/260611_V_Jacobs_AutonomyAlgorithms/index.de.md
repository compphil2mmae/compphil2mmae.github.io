abstract: |
  In Germany, the electronic patient record (ePA) aims to digitally store medical data and allow patients to independently manage and oversee their health information. With direct access to diagnoses and treatment records, patients can develop a better understanding of their medical situation and take an active role in managing it. However, it is unclear whether the ePA actually strengthens patients’ epistemic autonomy or if it creates new challenges. Drawing on the experience of implementing the electronic patient record in Germany, this talk considers what is needed to foster epistemic autonomy and re ects on which concept of epistemic autonomy is best suited to reviewing practical cases.
